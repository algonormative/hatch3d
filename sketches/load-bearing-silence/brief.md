# Load Bearing Silence

The third PHASE GARDEN concert study is a suspended brutalist crossing with its central member removed. Two angled, irregular remnants carry the composition across the page; a large paper void remains between their cut faces. That oversized central blank is intentional: it is the missing load, so filling it would erase the subject. One remnant supports broad, smooth lamellar sheets whose curved contours oppose hard portals and cut masonry. The first read is the diagonal structural interruption and its quiet void; at close range, the sheet growth and scores emerge. The drawing is an original visual response to system building, reconstructed intervals, and structural silence. It does not depict an actual concert or artist billing.

The five physical inks have separate jobs. Carbon draws load edges and densely scored masonry; ultramarine continues the cool beam planes and most sheet contours; vermilion marks stress at missing cuts; acid records sparse displaced intervals and sheet cross marks; violet threads through the softer growth. Their densities deliberately differ. An oblique orthographic camera exposes front faces, undersides, and detached portal fragments. Broad tapered ribbons carry 30 continuous longitudinal rails each and only five transverse marks, so they read as flowing sheets instead of another hatch plane. Every solid plane, including subdivided ribbon surfaces, enters one shared CPU depth pass before strokes are classified. The art is authored in x 18–279 mm and y 76–357 mm coordinates, then fitted to Tabloid portrait 279.4 × 431.8 mm (11 × 17 inches) on warm `#f4f0e6` paper. Abstract mode reclaims the lettering bands.

The **Composition** radar drives three native macros: Architectural load changes beam width and hatch spacing, Portal tension changes route bend and portal scale, and Disintegration changes gap width and branch reach. Branch root is an XY placement pad. Bridge world position moves the geometry in actual X, Y, and Z coordinates. The underlying scalar controls remain available for precise edits; the hidden-line toggle exposes the depth decision for inspection. Named seed streams vary route chirality and bend, gap location, detached portal arrangement, and ribbon curvature and reach while retaining the material grammar. All loops are finite; no shader or canvas compositing is needed.

Run `npm run sketch -- open sketches/load-bearing-silence/sketch.ts` to explore. A deterministic reference render is `node --import tsx cli/sketch.ts render sketches/load-bearing-silence/sketch.ts --seed 17 --out .sketch-output/phase-garden/load-bearing-silence/seed-17`. Matched seed 17 and 211 revisions were inspected before and after the growth pass. A first larger version was too angular; continuous S-curved rails now make the softer material legible. Seed 211 has a broader layered canopy, while seed 17 keeps a tighter knot near its roots. Both preserve the empty center. Dedicated tests check repeatability, plot bounds, central clearance, ink presence, and control extremes; physical pen density still needs a test plot.
