# Load Bearing Silence

A broken crossing orbits a load it cannot carry. The first read is a wide, luminous upper-left to lower-right sheet wrapping a small unmarked core; the second is a set of torn concrete members caught in that field. Rays leave in unequal fans with quiet angular wedges. A lamellar growth form under the lower remnant supplies a softer counterweight. The paper core stays open even when the light is strongest. This is an original abstract concert study, not an image of an actual venue or artist billing.

The crossing retains cut brutalist beams, sheared portals, and growth ribbons. Seeded satellite slabs now vary in orbit radius, size, angle, shear, thickness, and hatch, with interrupted arc inscriptions that face the same center. The singularity XY pad moves both the light and orbit system. Named random streams separate route, satellite topology, growth, ribbon deformation, and ray fans, so changing one control need not scramble every other decision. The high-power sheet has roughly 60–80 possible coherent rails, and rays can reach 120. The final quarter of each rail curls toward a different point along an arc around the core, rather than ending on one straight cut. A minimum rail spacing floor limits near-parallel pen overlap. Ray count governs actual rays; seeded fans vary angular width, length, curvature, and strength instead of filling the circle uniformly.

Eight physical pens carry distinct work: carbon for edges and load lines, ultramarine for cooler structural faces and growth, vermilion for stressed cuts, acid for displaced cuts and growth cross marks, violet for softer undersides and daughter sheets, plus three added pens: cyan for the dominant light field, hot coral for flares, and pale gold for sparse highlights. Palette variation changes seeded band boundaries and accent assignment while preserving cyan's larger share. The extra colors are actual plotter layers with stable pen IDs. Dense geometry is clipped to the authored art rectangle, then composed on warm 279.4 × 431.8 mm Tabloid stock. Optional poster lettering remains available; abstract mode reclaims its bands. Every solid, including satellite side faces and the growth sheets, participates in the shared CPU depth pass before stroke visibility is classified. The light is a view-aligned plane and does not occlude other strokes.

The Composition radar combines architectural load, portal tension, and singularity power. Power simultaneously widens and densifies the sheet and grows the ray field; the direct controls still allow a quiet or exaggerated setting. Explicit ribbon and ray toggles remain off even when the power macro is high. Separate controls govern structural density, irregularity, orbital fragmentation, ribbon amount/width/bend/pinch/extent, fan density/count/length/spread/curve, and palette variation. Shape ranges are substantially broader than the prior version; world and XY position controls keep their previous bounds. All loops are finite and geometry is deterministic for a seed and parameter set.

Run `npm run sketch -- open sketches/load-bearing-silence/sketch.ts` to explore. Seed 211 comparison renders and exact parameter JSON live under `.sketch-output/silence-power/`. The automated checks cover deterministic output, bounded finite Tabloid paths, physical ink allocation, density and seed variation, independent light toggles, compact open core, and slider extremes. The previews are visual checks; physical pen density and registration still need a test plot before calling this press-ready.
