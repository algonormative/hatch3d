# Load Bearing Silence

A broken crossing orbits a load it cannot carry. The first read is a cool, torn light sheet bending around an unmarked core; the second is a set of cut concrete members under pressure. Unequal ray fans and the soft lamellar growth below the lower remnant supply counterpoint. The core stays quiet even when the light is strongest.

The bridge route, portals, buttresses, orbiting fragments, and branching growth remain the structural drawing. Named seeded streams separate their topology from ribbon density, ink allocation, fan geometry, and the light gap field. The light lies on a view-aligned plane and participates in the same depth classification wherever it crosses the bridge. Outside that fixed camera viewport, there are no bridge occluders; those outer light fragments remain available for the final poster and finishing clips.

The ribbon's tone is made with marks: `ribbonShade` gently compresses rail spacing toward its center and drops more outer rails, while retaining a minimum useful plotted spacing. A smooth seeded two-dimensional field cuts irregular gaps across neighboring rails and rays; `lightGapAmount` sets how much is missing and `lightGapScale` sets the size of the coherent patches. Broken paths are emitted as separate polylines, so a pen never bridges a gap. The main light uses a muted blue-gray pen, with occasional warm-gray and sand accents assigned sparsely by rail rather than in adjacent color bands. All eight physical pens remain available for the whole composition, though they need not carry equal light work.

`ribbonEdgeReach` and `rayEdgeReach` independently extend outer light endpoints toward the available framed art boundary. At zero they retain their natural extents; at one, some paths can terminate at the finishing content limit. The bridge and lettering keep their existing transform. The sketch inverses the poster art fit for light alone, allowing abstract light to occupy the full frame and restricting lettered light to the band between header and footer. The final finishing pass controls borders, margins, and stroke-safe clipping; for a close inner-frame study use a double border with `inset: 12` and `contentGap: 0`. A larger content gap preserves a wider rest around the border.

The Composition radar combines architectural load, portal tension, and singularity power. Power widens and densifies the sheet and grows the ray field; independent ribbon and ray toggles remain authoritative. Direct controls govern shape, tone, gaps, and frame reach. Source geometry is finite and seeded. The preview renders are visual checks; physical pen density and registration still need a test plot before calling this press-ready.

Run `npm run sketch -- open sketches/load-bearing-silence/sketch.ts` to explore. Matched seed-211 previews and their exact parameter JSON are under `.sketch-output/silence-tonal-edges/`.
