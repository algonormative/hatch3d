# Breach Cathedral

An original pen-plotter poster about a system being interrupted by a living one. A monumental stack of cantilevered concrete-like plates frames a deliberately empty central shaft. A continuous ribbed membrane loops through it, crossing alternately in front of and behind the slabs. The first reading is a single architectural silhouette and its breach; close viewing reveals rhythmic cuts, contour laminations, and five physical ink systems.

The musical references are methods rather than visual samples: system-generated negative space, 64 grouped interruptions, changing harmonic mass, and the collision of hard constraint with complex living curvature. No instrument, waveform, album art, logo, or artist-specific motif is depicted.

A3 portrait; warm stock. The art stays within x = 18–279 mm and y = 76–357 mm, reserving the header and footer for the common Phase Garden frame. Carbon carries the major mass, ultramarine its internal shadow grammar, vermilion and violet the organic lamellae, and acid the rare active seam. Every mark is a plotted polyline. The CPU depth pass includes both slab solids and the membrane so overlaps are real geometric visibility decisions.

Controls group three broad composition axes, an organic focus XY that translates only the membrane, and a genuine XYZ placement that moves the whole scene. Seed 17 is the first proof. A changed seed reshuffles slab side, cantilever extent, interrupted 64-beat pattern, membrane phase, and independently chosen sweep, handedness, aspect, and tilt while retaining the same graphic language.
