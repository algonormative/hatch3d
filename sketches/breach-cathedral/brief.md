# Breach Cathedral

An original pen-plotter poster about a system being interrupted by a living one. A monumental stack of cantilevered concrete-like plates frames a deliberately empty central shaft. A continuous ribbed membrane loops through it, crossing alternately in front of and behind the slabs. The first reading is a single architectural silhouette and its breach; close viewing reveals rhythmic cuts, contour laminations, and five physical ink systems.

The musical references are methods rather than visual samples: system-generated negative space, 64 grouped interruptions, changing harmonic mass, and the collision of hard constraint with complex living curvature. No instrument, waveform, album art, logo, or artist-specific motif is depicted.

The physical sheet is 11 × 17 inch Tabloid (279.4 × 431.8 mm) on warm stock. Geometry is still authored in the original 297 × 420 mm coordinate system, with an art envelope of x = 18–279 mm and y = 76–357 mm. Shared composition maps that fixed envelope uniformly to the physical page: the lettered edition reserves a header and footer, while the abstract edition centers the art in the full sheet interior. Carbon carries the major mass, ultramarine its internal shadow grammar, vermilion and violet the organic lamellae, and acid the rare active seam. Every mark is a plotted polyline. The CPU depth pass includes both slab solids and the membrane so overlaps are real geometric visibility decisions.

Controls group three broad composition axes, an organic focus XY that translates only the membrane, and a genuine XYZ placement that moves the whole scene. Seed 17 is the first proof. A changed seed reshuffles slab side, cantilever extent, interrupted 64-beat pattern, membrane phase, and independently chosen sweep, handedness, aspect, and tilt while retaining the same graphic language.


## Expanded control study

The neutral seed-0 lettered export is unchanged at the path level. Direct controls now span five to thirteen slab levels, a 0.4–3.7 world-unit membrane width, and the full 0–1 interruption interval. Cantilever reach and void opening now alter the slab silhouette and aperture substantially; hatch density drives the front, side, and top courses as well as membrane contour and rib counts. The three composition axes have wider macro reach without changing their neutral positions. Organic focus, world XYZ, camera, page, and pen assignments retain their prior ranges.

Matched seed-0 whole-sheet previews show the sparse endpoint as a legible broken arch among widely spaced slabs; the packed endpoint pushes the membrane into large folded fields and turns the masonry into a heavy counterweight. The dense extreme intentionally risks crowding the central shaft: it is an expressive upper limit, not a default. Software checks confirm finite paths, the art aperture, the five ink layers, and a bounded point count. A physical plot is still needed to judge ink buildup at this extreme.
