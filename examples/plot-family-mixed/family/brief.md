# Double ring and paper curve

This is a copied migration fixture of `sketches/hatch3d-3d`, not a new selected artwork. Two torus rings and their connector share one CPU depth pass. The paper-space curve uses a second pen and does not occlude the solids. The copied family owns its two composition files; the original sketch remains in Hatch3D.

The default request fixes seed 5 and a simple frame, with two passes on the ring pen. Compare canonical SVG, identity, paths, ordered pen layers and finishing with the original before editing this copy. New work should change the relationship between depth and the paper curve, then review the whole sheet.
